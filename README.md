# Globa Prediction Markets Platform

A decentralized prediction market platform built on Stacks, enabling creation and trading of event outcome predictions with automated market making.

## Core Features

### Market System
- Binary (Yes/No) markets
- Scalar markets (ranged predictions)
- Categorical markets (multiple choice)
- Automated market making (AMM)
- 10-day resolution window

### Trading Parameters
- Minimum bet: 0.1 STX
- Maximum bet: 100K STX
- Market creation fee: 10 STX
- Minimum liquidity: 50 STX

### Fee Structure
- Trading fee: 2%
- Oracle fee: 1%
- Liquidity provider reward: 50% of fees

### Market Making
- Constant product AMM
- Automated price discovery
- Liquidity pool management
- Share minting/burning

### Social Features
- Predictor profiles
- Performance tracking
- Follow system
- Reputation scoring
